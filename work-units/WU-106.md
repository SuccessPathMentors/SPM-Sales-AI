# WU-106 — End-to-End Sales Journey & Multi-Turn Orchestration

Status: SPEC_DRAFT — SCOPE FREEZE V1
Issue: #65
Dependency: WU-105 `LOCKED` on `main`
Scope: STAGING certification only. Production activation/deployment is not authorized.

## 1. Objective
Certify that the locked Success Path Mentors conversation stack can carry a customer through realistic multi-turn sales/service journeys while preserving trusted context, current-message precedence, action safety, business truth, language continuity, and compatibility with WU-101 through WU-105.

WU-106 does **not** add a new intent taxonomy, new business policies, or a second conversation agent. It orchestrates and certifies transitions across the already-authoritative conversation stack.

## 2. Locked upstream inheritance
WU-106 must inherit without weakening:
- WU-101 conversation analytics/event compatibility;
- WU-102 unanswered-queue compatibility and current STAGING isolation controls;
- WU-103 locked release/safety behavior;
- WU-104 ambiguity clarification, awaited-context binding, correction precedence, stale-context override, multilingual clarification parity, loop cap, and action-safety behavior;
- WU-105 Golden Intent answer-first/no-reask/source/action contracts and CR-105-01 through CR-105-04.

Any required change to locked upstream behavior must be isolated as a versioned WU-106 Change Request and must not silently rewrite a prior lock.

## 3. Scope freeze
WU-106 V1 is limited to **end-to-end multi-turn orchestration and journey continuity**.

### In scope
1. Multi-turn trusted-state continuity.
2. Safe transition from one intent to another.
3. Current-message intent overriding stale prior sales context.
4. Known-field reuse and no unnecessary re-ask.
5. Explicit correction of grade/subject/location/day/time and related trusted fields.
6. Single-child and controlled child/customer-context switching.
7. Inquiry-to-action boundary handling.
8. Action-state honesty for availability, scheduling, registration, refund, and handoff.
9. Complaint/support/human-handoff interruption of a sales journey.
10. Language continuity and deliberate language switching across EN/AR/FR.
11. Resume-after-interruption behavior where safe.
12. Long-conversation regression and state hygiene.
13. Deterministic, privacy-safe journey telemetry/evidence.
14. Regression compatibility with locked WU-101 through WU-105.

### Out of scope
- changing the authoritative 62-intent taxonomy;
- adding new pricing, refund, trial, teacher-quality, cancellation, or scheduling policy;
- enabling live Production booking/registration/refund/payment/handoff actions;
- activating Production;
- creating a second LLM classifier/agent owned by WU-106;
- redesigning the website/chat UI;
- replacing WU-104 ambiguity logic;
- replacing WU-105 Golden Intent contracts;
- storing raw secrets, passwords, payment data, or unnecessary raw conversation payloads in WU-106 evidence.

## 4. P0 invariants
The following are release-blocking invariants:

**P0-01 — No known-field re-ask**
Trusted grade, subject, location, day, time, parent/student context, or other journey fields must not be re-requested unless they are genuinely missing, invalidated, ambiguous, stale-by-policy, or conflict with a current correction.

**P0-02 — Current message wins**
A clear current request/correction must override stale unrelated conversation state.

**P0-03 — No false live availability**
The assistant must not state that a slot/day/time is available without an authoritative live scheduling check.

**P0-04 — No false action success**
The assistant must not claim `booked`, `scheduled`, `registered`, `refunded`, `handoff completed/received`, or equivalent success unless the authoritative action actually succeeds.

**P0-05 — Safety/support override**
Support, complaint, opt-out/do-not-contact, and explicit human-help flows retain priority over sales optimization where their locked rules apply.

**P0-06 — No silent child-context contamination**
When the customer clearly switches from one child/student context to another, trusted fields from the previous child must not be silently applied to the new child.

**P0-07 — Locked upstream preservation**
WU-104 and WU-105 locked behaviors may not be weakened to make a WU-106 journey pass.

**P0-08 — Production boundary**
No WU-106 implementation or certification step may activate or modify the protected Production workflow.

## 5. Golden Journey set — V1
The initial certification set is frozen to the following 12 journeys.

### GJ-01 — Discovery → Pricing → Package Comparison
Example path: Grade/Subject inquiry → price → package differences.
Expected: reuse known grade/subject; source-backed price/package facts; no registration jump.

### GJ-02 — Discovery → Pricing → Price Objection → Trial
Example path: Math help → pricing → “too expensive” → interest in free trial.
Expected: natural objection handling, no invented discount, correct transition to explicit trial intake only when requested.

### GJ-03 — Trial Details → Explicit Trial Start → Registration Intake
Expected: distinguish information question from action request, preserve known student context, collect only the next missing field.

### GJ-04 — Registration → Availability → Schedule Request
Expected: registration intent can move to scheduling while keeping known grade/subject/customer context; availability inquiry does not equal booking.

### GJ-05 — Availability → Requested Slot → Safe Alternative
Expected: no false slot availability; if live execution is unavailable or slot is unconfirmed, remain explicit about the gate and request/offer the smallest next step.

### GJ-06 — Known Context → Single-Field Correction
Example: Grade 8 Math → “Actually Grade 9.”
Expected: replace corrected field, preserve compatible trusted fields, no unnecessary rediscovery.

### GJ-07 — Child/Student Context Switch
Example: discussion about son Grade 8 Math → “This is for my daughter in Grade 10 Physics.”
Expected: create/switch student context safely; do not carry incompatible son-specific fields into daughter context.

### GJ-08 — Sales Journey → Human Handoff
Expected: explicit human request overrides sales continuation; preserve conversation context; no false receipt/completion claim when automatic handoff is unavailable.

### GJ-09 — Sales Journey → Technical Support / Complaint Interruption
Expected: support/complaint takes priority; sales pressure stops; relevant prior context can be preserved for later safe resume without contaminating the support answer.

### GJ-10 — Policy Question Inside an Active Sales Journey
Example: pricing/trial journey → refund-policy question → return to previous sales context only if the customer continues it.
Expected: answer policy from approved source; do not turn policy inquiry into refund execution; do not lose prior trusted context.

### GJ-11 — Long Conversation → Stale-Context Override
Expected: after several turns, a new clear intent such as Physics, a new grade, or a different objective wins over stale pricing/trial state.

### GJ-12 — Multilingual Journey Continuity / Deliberate Language Switch
Minimum coverage: EN→AR, AR→EN, FR direct continuity, and same-language multi-turn continuation.
Expected: preserve semantic state while responding in the intended current language; do not duplicate intake merely because language changed.

## 6. State and transition contract — design principles
WU-106 will formalize an explicit journey-state contract before implementation.

Required principles:
- distinguish **trusted known state**, **current-message extracted state**, **awaited field**, **action-pending state**, and **historical/stale context**;
- current explicit corrections update the appropriate trusted field with traceable reason;
- a new child/student context must create a clear context boundary;
- action-pending state never equals action-success state;
- policy/information questions can temporarily interrupt a journey without silently changing the active business action;
- support/handoff interruptions may suspend sales progression;
- resume logic must never assume customer consent to continue an interrupted action;
- journey state must remain privacy-minimized and must not persist secrets.

## 7. Test strategy
Certification will use two layers.

### A. Deterministic/automated coverage
Planned coverage includes:
- Golden Journey transition matrix;
- known-field/no-reask assertions;
- correction precedence;
- child-context boundary tests;
- stale-context override;
- inquiry vs action-state invariants;
- support/handoff priority;
- multilingual transition fixtures;
- non-target intent compatibility;
- WU-101/WU-102/WU-104/WU-105 regression signatures;
- Production write/activation hard-stop.

### B. Owner-observed live STAGING journeys
Target: approximately **10–15 high-value multi-turn conversations**, not hundreds of manual screenshots.
Each live journey will have exact prompts, expected state transitions, PASS/FAIL criteria, and a clean-session/reuse-session instruction.

## 8. Acceptance criteria
- **AC-01** All 12 Golden Journeys have deterministic contracts and explicit expected transitions.
- **AC-02** Known trusted fields are reused and unnecessary re-asks are blocked.
- **AC-03** Clear current intent/correction overrides stale context.
- **AC-04** Single-field correction preserves compatible trusted context.
- **AC-05** Child/student context switching prevents cross-child contamination.
- **AC-06** Availability inquiry never implies live availability without source confirmation.
- **AC-07** Schedule request never implies booking success before action success.
- **AC-08** Registration intent/intake never implies registration success before action success.
- **AC-09** Refund-policy inquiry remains informational unless the user explicitly requests action; no eligibility/amount/timing is invented.
- **AC-10** Human-handoff request takes priority and never falsely claims receipt/completion.
- **AC-11** Support/complaint interruption safely suspends sales optimization.
- **AC-12** Language continuity/switching preserves semantic journey state across EN/AR/FR fixtures.
- **AC-13** Long conversations do not allow stale state to capture a new clear intent.
- **AC-14** Locked WU-104 ambiguity/correction controls remain intact.
- **AC-15** Locked WU-105 Golden Intent behavior remains intact.
- **AC-16** WU-101 analytics and WU-102 unanswered-queue compatibility remain intact.
- **AC-17** WU-106 evidence/state adds no secret or unnecessary raw-data persistence.
- **AC-18** Production remains untouched and inactive with respect to WU-106.
- **AC-19** Final deterministic regression passes on the exact candidate reviewed by the Owner.
- **AC-20** Owner-observed live journey set has no unresolved P0/P1 defect before final review.
